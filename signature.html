Outlook on the web (outlook.office.com)

  1. Click the gear icon, then Account, then Signatures.
  2. Click New signature and name it Wicked Heart.
  3. Click in the box and press Ctrl+V (Cmd+V on Mac).
  4. Under "Default signatures," pick it for new messages and replies, then Save.

Outlook desktop app

  1. File, Options, Mail, Signatures (new Outlook: Settings, Account, Signatures).
  2. Click New, name it, paste with Ctrl+V.
  3. Pick play@wickedhearthockey.com for new messages and replies, then OK.

iPhone Mail or Outlook phone app

  1. Phones strip the logo, so use the plain version below.
  2. iPhone: Settings, Apps, Mail, Signature. Outlook app: Settings, Signature.
